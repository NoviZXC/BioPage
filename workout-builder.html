Workout Builder

Build a simple structured run, download it as a workout file and copy it to your UNA Watch over USB. The Running app lists it for that day and the Workout Today glance shows it on the glances screen.

Everything runs in your browser. The file is built on your device; nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

Workout

The name shows on the watch. Plain letters and numbers fit best on the glances screen.

Warm-up
Work and rest

Work: one effort. Rest: the recovery after each effort (leave it as None for continuous repeats).

Cool-down

Your workout

      Copy it to the watch

      1. Download the file. It is named after the day, like 2026-11-03.fit. Keep that name: the watch finds today's workout by it.
      2. Connect the watch to your computer with its USB cable and open its drive.
      3. Next to the Apps folder, create a folder Workouts and inside it a folder Schedule (skip what already exists).
      4. Copy the file into Workouts/Schedule, so its full path is Workouts/Schedule/YYYY-MM-DD.fit. One file per day.
      5. Eject the drive. On that day the Running app lists the workout, and the Workout Today glance shows it.

      For now plans are copied over USB; sending them from the phone is coming from UNA. The watch needs firmware 1.6.0 or newer.

      A planning aid for your own training. It builds a workout file and does nothing else.
